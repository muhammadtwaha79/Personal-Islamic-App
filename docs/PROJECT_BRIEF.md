# Project Brief

## Vision

Ibadah Companion helps one person keep accurate, private records of their worship, recover missed obligations responsibly, and reflect with sincerity, without turning worship into a game.

## Priority scope (first release line)

1. Daily obligatory Salah tracking (five prayers, independent states).
2. Sunnah and Nafl tracking (Hanafi-oriented initial profile, user-confirmed).
3. **Qaza recovery** on an append-only ledger (the most important feature).
4. Ramadan and voluntary fasting with a configurable Hijri calendar.

## Later (not implemented, not designed in detail)

Dhikr, Salawat, Quran reading and memorization, Adhkar, Duas, Islamic knowledge, journaling, charity, Ramadan programs.

## Non-goals

- Issuing fatwas or resolving scholarly disagreements.
- Social features, leaderboards, public sharing.
- Cloud accounts, mandatory sync, advertising, analytics by default.
- Any metric framed as spiritual rank or acceptance of worship.

## Product philosophy (summary)

Sincerity over scores; accountability for obligations; gentle, non-pressuring improvement; peaceful, respectful design; religious accuracy over feature count. Streaks and percentages, where shown, are descriptive consistency information only and must carry no language of reward, rank or divine acceptance.

## Users and context

Single user, Android phone, frequent offline use, English and Roman Urdu UI (Urdu script with RTL later). Estimated historical Qaza of roughly one year is the motivating scenario; all such numbers are user estimates, never verified records.

## Success criteria for the foundation

- No loss or silent alteration of obligation records, ever.
- Reproducible cloud builds and tests through GitHub Actions.
- Every religious assumption is visible, versioned and reviewable.
